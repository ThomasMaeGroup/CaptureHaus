# Giraffe360 — product research notes

Gathered from giraffe360.com (Oct 2026). Reference only; the brand kit in `/kit` takes precedence.

## What it is
Real-estate listing media platform: a **capture camera** plus **Content Studio**, cloud software
that turns one property visit into a complete set of listing marketing.
Site meta: *"Real estate camera and AI software for versatile listing media."*
Hero: **"Market listings…"**, *"Everything you need to make every listing look its best, from enhanced
photos to videos, social content and your own websites."*

## Who it's for
Estate agents, brokerages/teams, real-estate photographers & media providers, luxury property marketers.
Trust line: **"Trusted by over 10,000 real estate photographers, agents, and brokerages"**, 4.7★ Trustpilot.
Logos: Savills, Knight Frank, CBRE, RE/MAX, Keller Williams, eXp Realty, HomeSmart, Belvoir, Reeds Rains.

## Three ways in ("Start with the media you have. Choose how you get the rest.")
1. **Work with a Giraffe360 photographer**: receive photos, tours, floor plans and video, then AI-edit.
2. **Capture it yourself** with the camera: guided capture collects visual, 360 and LiDAR data.
3. **Upload existing photography** into Content Studio to enhance it and build marketing from it.

## Outputs (from one capture)
- HDR photos (AI enhance, colour, blue skies)
- 360° HDR virtual tours (any device)
- 2D & 3D floor plans (LiDAR-measured, "appraiser-ready")
- Listing videos + short social clips
- Branded marketing: social posts, emails, flyers, brochures
- Property / agent websites: all media in one place, with enquiry capture

## AI tools ("Built-in AI tools to edit, stage, and create more from every capture.")
Virtual staging · twilight / sky replacement · furniture & object removal · drone-style aerial shots ·
Sunpath simulation (luxury).
