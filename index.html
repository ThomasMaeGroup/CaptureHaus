Ask a question
What exactly is a 360° virtual tour?

It's an interactive, walk-through view of your space made from high-resolution 360° images. Visitors click to move between rooms and drag to look around, on any phone, tablet or computer, or in a VR headset. Nothing to download.

What's the difference between 2D and 3D floor plans?

A 2D floor plan is the classic top-down drawing with room sizes and total floor area, ideal for listings and print. A 3D floor plan is an interactive model you can rotate to see how rooms connect. It's great for open-plan, multi-level and commercial layouts.

How accurate are the floor plans?

Very. Our camera uses LiDAR laser scanning to measure every room as it captures, so dimensions come from the scan data rather than a tape measure. Plans are drawn to recognised measurement standards.

How long does a capture take?

It depends on the size of the space, but most homes, restaurants and shops are captured in a single visit. Each room takes just a few minutes. We'll give you a time estimate with your quote.

Will it disrupt my business?

No. For restaurants, pubs and shops we can capture before opening or after closing. The camera works on its own once it's positioned, and the space just needs to be tidy and clear of people for each scan.

How quickly will I receive everything?

Fast. Photos, tours and floor plans are processed in the cloud straight after capture, and we'll confirm your delivery date when you book.

Where can I use the tour and floor plans?

Anywhere. Share by link, embed on your website, add to property portals that accept virtual tour links, post to social media, add to your Google Business Profile, or print the floor plans in brochures and window cards.

How do I prepare my space?

Treat it like a photo shoot: declutter surfaces, turn on all the lights, open blinds and curtains, and move cars off the drive. We'll send you a simple checklist before your capture.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the space and which outputs you need. Tell us a little about your project in the form and we'll send a clear, no-obligation quote. Multi-property and agency packages are available.

Which areas do you cover?

Get in touch with your postcode and we'll confirm availability. We regularly travel for larger projects and multi-site work.
